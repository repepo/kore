    Background model

    kore solves for small perturbations of a background in which the density \(\rho_0\), the pressure (or \(p_0 = \rho_0 T_0\)) and gravity \(g\) depend on \(r\) only, the entropy has a radial gradient \(dS/dr\), and the viscosity \(\nu\) and the thermal diffusivity \(\kappa\) are radial profiles. The background has three parts, each chosen by name in the parameter file:

    part parameter what it sets
    structure structure \(\rho_0\), \(p_0\) and \(g\)
    stratification stratification \(dS/dr\), with \(S = \delta s/c_p\) (only with thermal = 1)
    diffusivity diffusivity the radial profiles of \(\nu\) and \(\kappa\)

    The rotation of the background, uniform or differential, is chosen separately (differential-rotation.md). The code is in bin/background.py (the choice, the checks, the stratification and diffusivity types) and bin/radial_profiles.py (one class per structure). These names replaced model_type, heating, solar_rz, solar_density and aux0 on 2026-10-26; the last section translates old parameter files.

    Units

    kore is dimensionless. The unit of length is the radius of the outer wall, \(r = 1\). r_out is that radius in star radii (1 by default), so \(x = r\cdot\)r_out is the radius in star radii. With Gaspard = 1 the unit of time is \(1/\Omega\); then ViscosD \(= \nu/(\Omega L^2)\), ThermaD \(= \kappa/(\Omega L^2)\) with \(L\) = r_out star radii, and the buoyancy frequency is

    \[ N^2 = \mathrm{Beyonce}\thinspace g\thinspace \frac{dS}{dr} \]

    in units of \(\Omega^2\). The only physical unit kore reads is that of the solar rotation data, in nHz.

    bin/units.py converts physical values; kore itself never imports it. In a parameter file, after r_out is set:

    import units
units.set_physical(par, frame_nHz=456.0, nu=1e12, kappa=1e12)   # Gaspard, Beyonce, ViscosD, ThermaD, diff_rot_frame
units.set_target(par, omega=-271.2, sigma=-33.2)                 # itau, rtau from ω, σ in nHz

    set_physical takes \(\nu\) and \(\kappa\) in cm²/s, and the solar radius and surface gravity unless radius and gsurf (cgs) are given; Beyonce \(= g_{\rm surf}/(L\Omega^2)\) assumes \(g = 1\) at the surface, as in the solar structures. In a run directory, python3 bin/units.py prints the eigenvalues as \(\omega\) and \(\sigma\) in nHz in the rotating frame.

    Quick start

    The background of the smooth Sun example (examples/smooth_sun/parameters.py):

    par.thermal        = 1
par.r_out          = 0.985                 # outer wall in solar radii
par.structure      = 'model S hydrostatic'
par.stratification = 'solar_rz'            # a radiative interior below a slightly subadiabatic convection zone
par.solar_rz_delta_cz = -4e-7
par.diffusivity    = 'solar_rz'            # ν, κ drop to visc0, therm0 below 0.70 R☉
par.visc0, par.therm0 = 0.01, 0.04
units.set_physical(par, frame_nHz=456.0, nu=1e12, kappa=1e12)

    The checked-in bin/parameters.py, a gravito-inertial mode, uses structure = 'Wu 2005' with r_out = 0.99 and stratification = 'top hat'.

    Structure

    structure has no default.

    structure \(\rho_0\), \(p_0\), \(g\) parameters
    'model S hydrostatic' the Sun, for new runs: an even polynomial fit of \(\ln\rho\) of Model S (bin/model_s.at) over the whole Sun, \(g\) from its mass, \(p_0\) from hydrostatic balance structure_deg (30)
    'Wu 2005' \(\rho_0 = (1 - x^2)^2\) (Wu 2005, \(\beta = 2\)) with its \(g\) and \(p_0\) r_out < 1, so that \(\rho_0 > 0\) at the wall
    'Boussinesq' \(\rho_0 = p_0 = 1\), \(g = r\): the Boussinesq equations –
    '8-term fit' (legacy) the smooth fit of bin/smoothed_sun.py, up to 48 % off Model S in \(\rho\) near the surface –
    'old model S prototype' (legacy) Chebyshev fits of \(\ln\rho\) and \(\ln p\) of Model S, with the \(g\) of the 8-term fit (not hydrostatic) solar_density_deg, solar_density_window, solar_density_sun_deg
    • Only ratios of \(\rho_0\) and of \(p_0\) enter the equations, so their scale does not matter. \(g\) is 1 at the surface (\(x = 1\)) in the solar structures and in 'Wu 2005'.
    • The three solar structures (the first and the last two rows) also make \(x = r\cdot\)r_out the radius of the solar rotation profiles (diff_rot.solar_x).
    • 'model S hydrostatic' is within 1.0 %, 0.5 % and 0.75 % of Model S in \(\rho\), \(p\) and \(g\) at degree 30, and within 0.14 %, 0.06 % and 0.10 % at degree 50. Use 50 to compare with papers that use Model S itself; degrees of 40 and above need about 16 more lmax for the same \(\ell\)-convergence of the HFR modes (heat-equation.md §8, "Hydrostatic structure").
    • The two legacy structures are kept so that runs made before 'model S hydrostatic' give the same results. For 'old model S prototype', solar_density_window None fits the shell \([\)ricb\(\cdot\)r_out \(- 0.01\), r_out \(+ 0.005]\) when ricb > 0, and is 'sun' when ricb = 0: one even fit over the whole Sun of degree solar_density_sun_deg (60) (heat-equation.md §8, "Model S density").

    Stratification

    With thermal = 1, stratification sets \(dS/dr\) for \(r \ge 0\), in units of \(1/L\); it has no default then. In a full sphere (ricb = 0) kore uses it as an odd function of \(r\), so it should go to zero smoothly at the centre.

    stratification \(dS/dr\) parameters
    'top hat' \(\tfrac{A}{2}\left[\mathrm{erf}\frac{r - x_1}{w_1} - \mathrm{erf}\frac{r - x_2}{w_2}\right]\) aux1…aux5 = \(x_1\), \(w_1\), \(x_2\), \(w_2\), \(A\)
    'odd top hat' the same made odd in \(r\): with \(x_1 = 0\) it rises as \(A\thinspace\mathrm{erf}(r/w_1)\) from the centre aux1…aux5
    'solar_rz' the radiative interior of Mukhopadhyay et al. 2025b, below solar_rz_dSdr, solar_rz_steps, solar_rz_core, solar_rz_delta_cz, solar_rz_delta_sf, solar_rz_os
    'linear' \(r\); with 'Boussinesq', \(N^2\) = Beyonce \(r^2\) (Rieutord 2006, Mirouh et al. 2016) –
    'inverse square' \(1/r^2\), conduction between the walls needs ricb > 0
    'user' stratification_profile, a numpy expression (below) stratification_param (\(p\))
    • 'top hat' in a full sphere needs \(x_1 \gtrsim 6 w_1\), so that \(dS/dr \to 0\) at the centre; otherwise use 'odd top hat'. With ricb > 0 and ThermaD = 0, a \(dS/dr \neq 0\) at the inner wall gives spurious modes trapped there.
    • 'odd top hat' with aux1…aux5 = 0, 0.03, 0.68, 0.06, 2.6 fits the \(dS/dr\) of Model S to 10 % rms.

    'solar_rz'. With \(x = r\cdot\)r_out in solar radii (their eq. 10, plus the convection zone of Bekki et al. 2024, eq. 2):

    \[ \frac{dS}{dr} = \frac{A}{4}\left[1 - \mathrm{erf}\frac{x - x_1}{d}\right]\left[1 - \mathrm{erf}\frac{x - x_2}{d}\right]\mathrm{erf}\frac{x}{w_0} + \delta(x)\thinspace \frac{d\ln p_0}{dr}\thinspace \frac{1}{2}\left[1 + \mathrm{erf}\frac{x - x_{OS}}{d_{OS}}\right], \]
    \[ \delta(x) = \delta_{cz} + (\delta_{sf} - \delta_{cz})\thinspace e^{-((x - 0.985)/0.015)^2}, \]

    with \(A\) = solar_rz_dSdr (2.615), \([x_1, x_2, d]\) = solar_rz_steps ([0.61, 0.68, 0.04]), \(w_0\) = solar_rz_core (0.05: makes \(dS/dr\) odd and smooth at \(r = 0\), no effect above \(x \approx 3w_0\)), \([x_{OS}, d_{OS}]\) = solar_rz_os ([0.70, 0.04]) and \(\delta_{cz}\), \(\delta_{sf}\) = solar_rz_delta_cz, solar_rz_delta_sf (0). \(A\) is their \((\partial s_0/\partial r)_{RZ}\) = 1.32e-2 erg g⁻¹ K⁻¹ cm⁻¹ times \(L/c_p\), with \(c_p\) = 3.46e8 and r_out = 0.985, so it scales with r_out. \(\delta < 0\) is subadiabatic; kore stops if \(\delta\) is set with another stratification. The fits of \(\delta_{cz}\) to the observed HFR frequencies are in heat-equation.md §8.

    Diffusivity

    \(\nu\) = ViscosD \(\hat\nu(r)\) and \(\kappa\) = ThermaD \(\hat\kappa(r)\); diffusivity sets \(\hat\nu\) and \(\hat\kappa\).

    diffusivity \(\hat\nu\), \(\hat\kappa\) parameters
    'uniform' (default) 1 –
    'step' visc0, therm0 below \(r\) = aux3, 1 above, joined by an erf of width aux4 visc0, therm0, aux3, aux4
    'solar_rz' visc0, therm0 below \(x_{OS}\), 1 above, as \(\tfrac12[1 + \mathrm{erf}((x - x_{OS})/d_{OS})]\) (Mukhopadhyay et al. 2025b, eqs. 8–9) visc0, therm0, solar_rz_os
    'user' diffusivity_profiles = {'nu': '...', 'kappa': '...'}, numpy expressions (below) diffusivity_param (\(p\))
    • With 'step' and 'solar_rz', ViscosD and ThermaD are the values above the step (the convection zone of the Sun). Mukhopadhyay et al. 2025b use visc0 = 0.01 and therm0 = 0.04.
    • Any type is multiplied by \((\rho_0(x_2)/\rho_0)^p\), \(x_2\) = aux3, with \(p\) = visc_pow for \(\hat\nu\) and therm_pow for \(\hat\kappa\): 0 (the default) keeps the kinematic diffusivities as set, 1 makes \(\rho_0\nu\) and \(\rho_0\kappa\) follow the profile instead.
    • Forcing 11 (random Reynolds stresses) uses the turbulent part of the viscosity, ViscosD \((\hat\nu - \nu_f)\): the floor \(\nu_f\) is visc0 with 'step' and 'solar_rz' when visc0 < 1 and 0 otherwise, unless rsf_nu_floor is set.

    'user' profiles

    stratification_profile and the two diffusivity_profiles are numpy expressions of r (the kore radius), x (as in the rotation profiles: \(r\cdot\)r_out with a solar structure, \(r\) otherwise) and p, with the functions listed for diff_rot_profiles in differential-rotation.md (exp, tanh, erf, abs, where, …). For example:

    par.stratification = 'user'
par.stratification_profile = '2.5*exp(-((r - 0.75)/0.06)**2)'      # dS/dr in 1/L for r >= 0
par.diffusivity = 'user'
par.diffusivity_profiles = {'nu': '0.01 + 0.99*0.5*(1 + tanh((x - 0.70)/0.015))', 'kappa': '1'}

    kore evaluates them at \(\lvert r\rvert\), so in a full sphere \(dS/dr\) is odd and \(\hat\nu\), \(\hat\kappa\) are even whatever the expression; for a profile that is smooth at the centre, \(dS/dr\) should still vanish there. The expressions must not contain #: spin_doctor writes them at the end of each params.dat row, after a #.

    Checks and output

    Before anything is built, kore stops on a background that cannot work: an unknown type, one of the former names below, 'Wu 2005' without \(0 <\) r_out \(< 1\), 'inverse square' with ricb = 0, \(\delta\) without 'solar_rz', or a 'user' expression that does not evaluate. submatrices.py also warns about any name in the parameter file that kore does not define, so a misspelt name is not ignored without notice.

    spin_doctor writes the background to params.dat (the full list of columns is in differential-rotation.md, Output). The columns, counted from 1:

    • 19 r_out, 20–24 aux1…aux5, 25 visc0, 34 therm0, 35 visc_pow, 36 therm_pow;
    • 63 structure, as its index in background.STRUCTURES: '8-term fit' 0, 'old model S prototype' 1, 'model S hydrostatic' 2, 'Wu 2005' 3, 'Boussinesq' 4;
    • 64 the degree of the Model S fit (structure_deg for 'model S hydrostatic'), 65–66 the window of the 'old model S prototype' fits (nan: the default; 'sun': ∓(r_out + 0.005)), 67 solar_rz (1 when the stratification is 'solar_rz'), 68–74 its parameters, 77–78 \(\delta_{cz}\), \(\delta_{sf}\);
    • 81 stratification and 82 diffusivity, as their index in background.STRATIFICATIONS ('top hat' 0, 'odd top hat' 1, 'solar_rz' 2, 'linear' 3, 'inverse square' 4, 'user' 5; −1 without a stratification) and background.DIFFUSIVITIES ('uniform' 0, 'step' 1, 'solar_rz' 2, 'user' 3);
    • the 'user' expressions, at the end of the row after a # that np.loadtxt skips (a row with nothing to note has no #).

    Columns 1–80 have the positions they had before these names, so scripts that read params.dat by column keep working.

    The former names

    Until 2026-10-26 the background was chosen by model_type, with heating, solar_rz, solar_density and aux0. These names now stop kore with the replacement; the matrices are bitwise the same in either spelling.

    former setting structure stratification diffusivity
    model_type = 'user def' 'Wu 2005' 'top hat' 'uniform'
    model_type = 'smoothed sun', solar_rz = 0 '8-term fit' (solar_density = 'fit') or 'old model S prototype' ('model S'), or the structure it set 'odd top hat' 'step'
    model_type = 'smoothed sun', solar_rz = 1 as above 'solar_rz' 'solar_rz'
    model_type = 'Boussinesq' 'Boussinesq' heating 'internal': 'linear'; 'differential': 'inverse square'; 'top hat': 'top hat' 'uniform'

    aux0 is r_out. Structure 'Boussinesq' was called 'uniform' from 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-28, and that name stops kore too. The file-based models ('poly', 'mesa', 'gsm', 'astropy table') were removed; they had not worked on this branch.
