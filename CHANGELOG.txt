CHANGELOG - kore, main branch
=============================

Newest first. Each entry names the commit; `git show <commit>` gives the full message. The branch grew out of
anelastic_log (variable density, entropy, solar background, differential rotation), merged with anelastic_mag (the
magnetic field), centrifugal (the deformed body) and, in phases, everything of conductive_IC.


2026-11-11
----------
New: Ncheb(Ek, rule) with named resolution rules: 'default' (17 Ek^-0.2, as before, so existing parameter files give
the same N), 'magnetic' (4.55 Ek^-0.242, conductive_IC's fit for torsional modes in a shell) and 'ekman' (the Ekman
layer of a viscous full sphere, 320 (Ek/1e-6)^-1/4), or a pair (a, p) for a Ek^-p; the solver guide has a section on
choosing N and lmax with the findings behind each rule.
New: this CHANGELOG.

2026-11-11  f769e459
--------------------
From conductive_IC:
- magnetic.dat has 21 columns in every run (the conditional ones written as 0): 17-20 the magnetic torques on the
  mantle and the inner core, 21 brms, the r.m.s. of the physical radial field on the outer wall (over the sphere and a
  period, in the unit of B0). spin_doctor prints |Γ|mag and |Γ|magIC again.
- tests/ci: the thermo-compositional onset of Silva, Mather & Simitev 2019 (Pr = Sc) and the gravito-inertial modes of
  Dintrans, Rieutord & Valdettaro 1999.
- koreviz: kmode(field='b', ic=True, mantle=True) gives the field inside the inner core (conducting, Bessel, or the
  potential field of an insulating core) and beyond the outer wall (resolved mantle, then the potential field), drawn
  with the fluid by merid, equat and surf.
Fixes in koreviz: a full-sphere solution was expanded twice and took the flow's parity for b; plots run on
matplotlib 3.10.
