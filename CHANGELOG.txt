CHANGELOG - kore, main branch
=============================

Newest first. Each entry names the commit; `git show <commit>` gives the full message. The branch grew out of
anelastic_log (variable density, entropy, solar background, differential rotation), merged with anelastic_mag (the
magnetic field), centrifugal (the deformed body) and, in phases, everything of conductive_IC.


2026-11-18
----------
The nonlinear background flow at any lmax, and continued in Ro (bin/bgflow.py). Newton's method passes all lmax + 1
degrees of its iterate into each step, but the profile codes of the background allowed degrees up to 35 only, so
`bgflow.py nmax nonlinear` stopped at once for lmax >= 35. Above degree 35 the code is now 'W', the letter and the
degree in three digits (Wt037), up to degree 999; every label up to degree 35 is unchanged (utils.decode_label,
radial_profiles). A Newton step is also accepted, with a warning, once an iteration shrinks the update less than twice
while it is below 1000 tol: the round-off floor of the dense solves, 1e-9 to 7e-8 at lmax 95 (N 48 to 128), sat above
tol = 1e-9 and the run ended at the 30-iteration exit. The file of a nonlinear run also holds Newton's converged
iterate and the parameters it depends on, and `bgflow.py nmax nonlinear START.npz` continues from it to the run's Ro
(5 iterations instead of 15 from Ro 0.42 to 0.40 at N 128); it stops if the other parameters differ. Tests:
tests/bgflow degree37 (replacing stop_degree) and newton_start. Docs: docs/couette-flow.md, The nonlinear flow, with
the m = 3 onset of Hollerbach (2003) at Ek 1e-4, ricb 1/3 (Ro_c 0.4195 at N 128, his 0.414; 0.45 on the linear flow).


2026-11-16
----------
The ℓ-convergence now includes the highest toroidal degree, and the field has its own. For a vector field,
utils4pp.identify takes the larger amplitude (the sum of the moduli of the Chebyshev coefficients) of the two highest
degrees, one of P and one of T, relative to the dominant degree. It used to take the highest degree alone, which has
only P for m = 0, symm = 1 and for m > 0, symm = -1 (for the field, by its own symmetry). P's coefficients are about l
times smaller than T's at the same energy, so the value was too small there: 60-600 times in the runs checked, for
inertial and torsional modes alike. Where the highest degree is T's (m = 0, symm = -1; m > 0, symm = 1), and for a
scalar (the entropy or the composition with hydro = 0), the value is unchanged. With magnetic = 1, spin_doctor also
gives the field's ℓ-convergence: magnetic.dat column 22 (lconv_b; 0 for a field that is zero) and, with the flow, the
table column ℓ-Conv 𝐛, right after ℓ-Convergence (the columns after it move one place right). Accept a mode when both
values are below 1e-4. On 13 torsional modes (Ek 1e-10, Em 1e-9, lmax 127-255, conducting inner core, thin wall), the
relative error of the eigenvalue was 0.01-0.1 times the field's ℓ-convergence. magnetic.dat had 21 columns before:
start a new one rather than append to an older one, as for params.dat (np.loadtxt and tools/get_data.py stop at the
first longer row). Apart from the ℓ-convergence values and the new columns, every output is the same to the last bit,
and a run under MPI writes what a serial run writes.

2026-11-16
----------
spin_doctor.py shares the solutions among MPI ranks: `mpiexec -n NP python3 bin/spin_doctor.py NP` (mpi4py). Rank k takes
the solutions k, k + NP, ... and reads only their columns of the solution files (field_io.count gives their number);
rank 0 gathers the results, prints the table and writes the files, the same to the last bit as a serial run's but for
the run time in params.dat. MPI is used when mpiexec starts more than one rank, or with SD_MPI=1 (srun); SD_MPI=0 runs
serially. An error on one rank stops them all.
Faster post-processing, serial too. 20 torsional modes at N 1000, lmax 127 (conducting inner core, thin wall) on mongo:
147 s before, 42 s now, 17 s on 4 ranks and 9 s on 20. The sums over the degrees onto the (r, θ) grids of
viscous_dissipation, lorentz_enstrophy and magnetic_powers_grid are matrix products (utils4pp.synth_grid), which moves
their results at round-off: of the printed columns only residuals change, by about 1e-15 of their scale or less. What
does not depend on the solution is computed once, with the same numbers: the profiles of set_radii for each set of
radii, the Gauss-Legendre nodes (utils.leggauss), the Chebyshev polynomials of cheb2space_table at its radii; and
utils.Dcheb's recurrence runs as two cumulative sums instead of a loop over the coefficients (bitwise the same).

2026-11-11
----------
New: Ncheb(Ek, rule) with named resolution rules: 'default' (17 Ek^-0.2, as before, so existing parameter files give
the same N), 'magnetic' (4.55 Ek^-0.242, conductive_IC's fit for torsional modes in a shell) and 'ekman' (the Ekman
layer of a viscous full sphere, 320 (Ek/1e-6)^-1/4), or a pair (a, p) for a Ek^-p; the solver guide has a section on
choosing N and lmax with the findings behind each rule.
New: this CHANGELOG.
