# Checks of the centrifugal deformation

Companion of `docs/background.md` (Centrifugal deformation) and `docs/centrifugal-equations.md`, for `centrifugal = 1`
(`bin/centrifugal.py`, NEW 2026-11-05; with a field, phase 1b). `run_all.sh [WORKDIR]` builds the runs it needs under
`tests/centrifugal/runs/` and prints one verdict per check and its time (about 7 minutes on 16 cores; the budgets need
petsc4py and slepc4py, `SKIP_SOLVE=1 run_all.sh` skips them: 5 minutes on 16 cores, 10 on four; with `OLD_KORE` set to a
copy of the commit before the change it also runs `tests/diff_rot/regress.sh`, 2 minutes more). The times of the budget
checks leave out their runs, which `mk_run.py` builds and solves first.

| script | what it checks |
| --- | --- |
| `derive.py` | the tool: from the definitions alone (the map X = α + ξ, ξ = ε(r)∇(r²P2/2), and w = J F⁻¹u), with sympy in Cartesian components, the closed forms that `centrifugal.py` implements: the level surfaces r(1 + εP2); the identities that hold for any map (mass conservation, the covariant Coriolis force (F⁻¹ẑ)×w, the advection w_r S0′/J, the buoyancy Φ′(r) r̂); the first-order (M − I)w, (z̃ − ẑ)×w and J − 1; the map of `to_physical`; the effective potential on the level surfaces, whose monopole gives g − (2/3)Ω²r (a few seconds) |
| `derive_mag.py [--quick]` | the same for a field: from b = F b̃/J and B0 = F B̃0/J alone (sympy), the covariant Lorentz force (j̃ × B̃0 + J̃0 × b̃)/J with j̃ = ∇×(M b̃), the induction ∂ₜb̃ = ∇×(w × B̃0/J), their first-order parts δ𝐆 and δ𝐄, and the flux and tangential field of a potential field on the deformed wall; then the couplings of `centrifugal.magnetic_build` against the projection of δ𝐆 and δ𝐄 on kore's rows, block by block, and the wall terms against closed forms (the blocks are derived in parallel on up to 16 cores: two and a half minutes on 16, eight on four, a quarter of an hour on one; `--quick` two of the seven cases) |
| `bc_check.py` | the insulating-wall rows of `centrifugal.wall_coefficients` against the exact matching on the deformed wall: a potential field with random coefficients outside the CMB (or inside an insulating inner core), its flux and tangential field in the reference space computed pointwise with the exact F; the rows leave a residual of second order in f (it falls by 4 when f is halved), those of the sphere one of first order; m = 0, 1, 2, 5 (a few seconds) |
| `algebra_check.py [--quick]` | the angular couplings of `centrifugal.primitives` (the recurrence algebra of `bin/angular.py`) against a projection of the closed forms by Gauss-Legendre quadrature, with harmonics from `scipy.special.lpmv`: every term, m = 0–6, l up to 36, and the P2 coupling of the heat rows (5e-14) |
| `clairaut_check.py` | the flattening of `'clairaut'`: a homogeneous body (f = 5q/4, η = 0), the polytrope of index 1 (f/q = 7.5/π²), `'Wu 2005'` against Clairaut's second-order equation integrated from ρ itself (7e-10), the profile codes against finite differences and their Chebyshev fits, and the Sun (`'model S hydrostatic'`: f/q = 0.519) |
| `eig_check.py` | eigenvalues of small runs (scipy shift-invert on A.npz, B.npz): f = 0 gives the matrices of `centrifugal = 0` entry by entry; the spin-over of a Maclaurin spheroid (inviscid, uniform f) has (Im λ − 1)/f → 1 (exact 1 + f + f²/2, first order 1 + f + f²/3); the relabelling test (f = c(1 − r²)², a sphere labelled differently inside: 13 resolved modes move at O(c²), their odd part is O(c³)); `'clairaut'` of a homogeneous body equals `'uniform'` f = 5q/4; the monopole equals Beyonce(1 − 2q/3) for g = r; the stratified, deformed ideal fluid has imaginary eigenvalues (1e-16). With a field: f = 0 gives the matrices of `centrifugal = 0` (a conducting inner core with the axial field, a full sphere with general poloidal and toroidal fields); the relabelling test with the axial and the Malkus field, against the sphere with the field carried by the map (h = (1 + ε) r/2, and k1 = r(1 + η/5), k3 = −ηr/5): on the resolved modes the shifts agree to 1–2 % (axial) and 3–4 % (Malkus), the remainder being the magnetic diffusion kept spherical, which shrinks with Em |
| `bin/ru_insane.py` | with `centrifugal = 1` also: the metric blocks (inertia, Coriolis, entropy) packed as `assemble.py` packs them, against direct (r, cos θ) quadratures of the closed forms on random fields, the inertia and entropy blocks Hermitian with I + Ic and E + Ec positive, the Coriolis block anti-Hermitian, and KE of the deformed body against `utils4pp.centrifugal_terms` (round-off: 1e-14); `ru_wu_dr` adds a differential rotation with its thermal wind, which keeps the structure's g while the buoyancy takes the effective one |
| `budget_check.py [tol] [HALF]` | after `spin_doctor.py`: residσ, residω and resid𝑠 of every solution below tol (1e-8), also with a differential rotation (`bud_wu_dr`). spin_doctor weights KE with M, the Coriolis power with z̃ and TE with J by quadrature, apart from the matrices; without these weights the residuals are 1e-3 to 3e-2. With a field also resid𝐛 below tol, and with HALF (the same case at half the flattening) resid𝐄 of second order in f: the `bm_*` runs (uniform f, a conducting inner core, a full sphere with Luo_S2, the Malkus field, general fields with a differential rotation; residσ and residω below 1e-13, resid𝐛 below 3e-12, orders 1.7 to 2.0), and `bm_wu` (anelastic, `'clairaut'`, N 128: 2e-8, set by its resolution). Also solve.py's normalisation, KE = 1 and with a field KE + Hendrik (ME + MEc) = 1 (to the ten digits that the energies are printed with) |
| `fcn.sh [WORKDIR] [NP]` | the free core nutation, the spin-over of a fixed mantle, with a field: Ek 1e-7, Em 1e-5, Le 3e-3, the axial field and a conducting inner core, at the flattenings 0, f/2 and f = 2.5e-3 (N 160), without the field, and at N 200. Im λ moves by f(1 + f/3), as in the first-order equations, up to the part that the viscosity and the field add (1e-4 of f); the field changes the shift by 3e-5 of f, N 200 by 5e-6. Not in `run_all.sh`: 15 ranks (NP 1, 3, 5 or 15), 5 GB, about two minutes; MUMPS needs more workspace (`st_mat_mumps_icntl_14` = 500, set in the script). The budgets of these runs need more resolution (residσ 5e-4 at N 160, 1e-5 at N 200, also at f = 0) |
| `mk_run.py NAME 'par.x = ..' [solve]` | builds a run directory from the repo's `bin/` with the test defaults (the inviscid Boussinesq full sphere, m 1, symm −1, N 24, lmax 20, `'uniform'` f = 0, one rank) and runs submatrices and assemble (with `solve` also solve.py and spin_doctor.py) |

Results of 2026-11-05 are in `docs/centrifugal-equations.md` (Checks). What the checks do not reach: the metric terms that are
left out (the viscous force, the thermal and magnetic diffusion, the stress-free rows and the differential rotation of the
deformed body), whose size is estimated there.
